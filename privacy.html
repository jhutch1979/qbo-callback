Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-29

This application is a private, internal tool used only by Sunnies Up North LLC (Michigan). It is not offered to the public.

What the app does

The app imports Sunnies Up North LLC's own daily Clover POS sales summaries into its own QuickBooks Online company as journal entries.

Data accessed

The app accesses only the QuickBooks Online data of that single company.

Data sharing

No data is shared with third parties, sold, or used for advertising.

Credential storage

Credentials are stored in restricted-permission files on a private computer.

Disconnecting

You can disconnect the app at any time through the QuickBooks app settings.
